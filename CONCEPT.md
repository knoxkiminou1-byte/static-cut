**Tagline:** "Cut the signal. Survive the silence."
**Hook:** "A five-minute shooter where pressing MUTE rewrites the battlefield — and the final boss learns how you play."

## 1. WHY IT WINS
One instantly explainable verb — MUTE the entire game. Silence changes enemy behavior, freezes/worsens threats, physically crushes the audiovisual world. At ~4:35 the Station Voice plays back the player's own recorded MUTE rhythm as its attack pattern. Tell-your-friend: "The final boss fights you using your own rhythm."

## 2. CORE LOOP
WASD move, mouse aim/fire, hold Space to MUTE. MUTE builds Heat; release cools. 2.5s hold → overheat → 3s lockout. Five bands: (1) Calibration — Screecher/Tracker teaching; (2) Rhythm Fault — Drummer stored timing danger; (3) Feedback Loop — prolonged silence dangerous; (4) Signal Failure — contradictory combos; (5) Station Voice — three-phase boss reusing one recording system (firing rhythm→projectiles; movement→sweeping vectors; MUTE timing→arena signal cuts). 52 enemies/run. Enemy interactions enable mastery (Tracker charges strike enemies; Feedback blasts strip Screecher shields).

## 3. CHARACTERS

## 4. ART
Decommissioned broadcast laboratory. Charcoal #11120F, dirty bone #D8D0B8, oxidized brass, phosphor green #7FA66A, vermilion #D94A32. IBM Plex Mono + Atkinson Hyperlegible. Minute 1 rich CRT → minute 5 visual starvation. Signature MUTE: ~120ms crush toward horizontal signal axis. Category-swap proof.

## 5. SOUND
